All against Brent. Crude costs in the model are delivered costs including freight, which is why the light sweet column looks punishing for Europe specifically.

The three crude columns in the model correspond to the clusters above: light sweet is West African, North African, North Sea and US shale; medium sour is the Persian Gulf plus Russian Urals; heavy sour is Canadian oil sands, Venezuelan Orinoco, Mexican Maya and the heavier Gulf grades.

Yields are representative rather than plant-specific; real refineries blend several crudes and tune yields seasonally. Hydroskimming is distillation plus treating — whatever the crude naturally contains is what you sell. Cracking adds a vacuum unit and an FCC. Complex adds a hydrocracker and a coker, which destroy the residue almost entirely and leave petcoke as the only waste stream.
